DealHound — a Black Friday 2026 deal finder with 72 verified deals across 8 stores (Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart, Dell, Samsung, Google Store, Caraway, HexClad) and 6 categories, each price checked on 27 Nov 2026 with its source and an honest verdict (18 new lows, 50 strong prices, 4 routine sales). Search and filters by category, store and discount that work; sorting by best deal, biggest discount, lowest price or biggest saving; a score on every deal; and the same product compared across stores (AirPods Pro 3 at Amazon, Walmart and Best Buy; Galaxy S26 Ultra at Amazon and Samsung). Every discount is computed from the sale price and the usual price shown. The site is plain static files under app/ with no build step and no network request at runtime.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```
